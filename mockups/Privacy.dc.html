Privacy policy.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Information we collect

To set up and run a Plan, we collect your name, contact details, payment information, and details about the loved one each Plan is for. When you request a service, we also collect the information needed to coordinate it, such as location and scheduling details.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to process contributions, keep your dashboard accurate, send payment reminders, assign a photographer or videographer when a service is requested, and respond to your enquiries.

3. Sharing with photographers & videographers

When you request a service, we share only the information a matched photographer or videographer needs to carry it out — such as location and the service's date and scale. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Payment data

Card and bank details are handled by our payment processing partners; Journey Home does not store full card numbers. Every contribution is dated, recorded, and receipted, and is visible to you in your dashboard at any time.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No system is completely secure, and we work continuously to improve ours.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to our record-keeping obligations for active Plans and completed services.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to Plan holders in advance of taking effect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent to hello@journeyhome.ng or via our Contact page.
