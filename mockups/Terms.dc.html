Plan terms & conditions.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. The service

Journey Home lets you set up one or more savings plans ("Plans"), each tied to a named loved one, toward future photography and videography coverage of that person's burial service. A Plan has no fixed term or end date — contributions continue for as long as you choose, until the service is requested.

2. Contributions

You choose a monthly contribution amount and a package (Basic, Standard, or Premium) when you start a Plan. Every contribution is dated, recorded, and receipted the moment it is received, and is visible in your dashboard at any time.

3. Missed payments & deficits

If a scheduled contribution is missed, nothing on your Plan is forfeited. Instead, the missed amount is recorded as a deficit. You clear a deficit at your next convenient payment, alongside your regular contribution, whenever that works for you — there is no penalty for the time it takes to clear it.

4. Changing or adding plans

The package you choose (Basic, Standard, or Premium) is fixed for the life of a Plan and cannot be changed once that Plan has started. If your needs change, you may close the Plan and start a new one at the package you want — your saved balance on a closed Plan is handled as set out in Section 6. You may hold more than one Plan at a time, each tracked separately within your account.

5. Requesting the service

When the service is needed, you submit a service request through your dashboard. We assign a photographer or videographer from our verified network, matched to your state and the scale of the service. Delivery includes a digital gallery and, depending on package, a printed album.

6. Cancellation & refunds

You may close a Plan at any time. Balances saved toward a Plan that is closed before a service is requested are refundable, subject to verification, minus any outstanding deficit on that Plan.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to Plan holders in advance of taking effect.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to hello@journeyhome.ng or via our Contact page.
